## 5. Why Singularity (culture & values)

1. **Learn by Doing.** We build, prototype, and explore real-world problems through research-driven projects, hackathons, and innovation sprints.
2. **Multi-Domain Labs.** Whether you're into AI, Quantum, Robotics, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity & Blockchain, or AR/VR, there's a place for you. Cross-domain projects are encouraged and supported.
3. **Collaborate with the Best.** Work with passionate students, dedicated mentors, and industry leaders. We're supported by the Microsoft Student Community and growing partnerships every semester.
4. **Research with Impact.** From publishing papers to building MVPs, we incubate ideas that can evolve into startups, research publications, or community-focused solutions.

## 9. Joining / Recruitment

**Intro:**
> We don't run open enrollment. Singularity recruits through dedicated drives: a focused window where you apply, get evaluated, and join a lab that matches what you actually want to build.

**Process:**
1. **A recruitment drive opens.** Announced across our socials and on campus. It is not a rolling application but a defined window.
2. **You pick a lab.** Choose the domain that fits you: AI/ML, Quantum Computing, Robotics & IoT, Cybersecurity & Blockchain, Web & Cloud, AR/VR & Game Dev, or Interdisciplinary Research.
3. **You're evaluated and placed.** Selected students join their lab's team and start working on real projects alongside current members and executives.

**Current status:** Opens soon. Not currently accepting applications.
> The next recruitment drive hasn't been scheduled yet. Follow our Instagram and LinkedIn; that's where it'll be announced first.
